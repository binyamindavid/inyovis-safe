# Manual retention review and export

An archive admin opens a cleared, confirmed Business document and reads its retention decision, obligations, rule sources, recorded facts, and history. A missing filing date may leave a known lower bound; enter the verified filing date in the tax-year facts and reassess. Rule coverage must be validated for the archive country, entity, document type and applicable dates. A Personal reminder is an in-app date chosen by a member and does not create statutory retention.

Admins can place multiple reasoned holds. Each hold records who placed it and when. Release each hold individually with a reason; the actor, timestamp and release reason remain visible in history. A pending or stale decision, unresolved obligation, unvalidated scope, active hold, uncleared document or inclusive keep-through date blocks shredding review. The review opens no earlier than the next calendar day and checks current inputs and permissions again. Opening it leaves the original in the archive. **Shred document** is reserved for a future permanent deletion action.

For evidence export, give the reviewer the explicit bulk-export grant, then create and download the archive export. The ZIP includes confirmed metadata and retention state, confirmed revision ID, decision ID, decision revision ID, rule-set reference, keep-through date and Personal reminder in `index.csv`. A pending or stale row retains its state; the decision ID points to the latest preserved snapshot where present. The original PDF is included under `originals/`; the export is time-limited and access is checked again at download. Keep the ZIP and manifest in an authorised evidence store.

For account closure and eventual disposal, inventory active holds, confirmed originals, previews and derived objects, object-store protections, issued exports and their expiry, and backups. Decide a separate deletion policy for each copy, then verify deletion and record the actor, time, document and object identifiers, decision evidence, export handling and backup expiry in a durable deletion record. This review feature does not delete originals, previews, exports or backups.

A rollback that would erase holds, reminders or carried-forward rule identities is rejected before changing schema. Preserve that evidence and ship a forward repair. Migration 24 likewise refuses to remove release reasons after any hold has been released.
